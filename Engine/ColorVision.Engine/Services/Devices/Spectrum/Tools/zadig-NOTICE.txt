GCS spectrometer installation:
1. Open Options -> List All Devices.
2. Select GCS Spectrameter. Verify the device name before continuing.
3. Select libusb-win32 on the right side of the driver arrow.
4. Select Replace Driver.

Warning: Never replace the driver of a keyboard, mouse, receiver, camera, or USB hub.
