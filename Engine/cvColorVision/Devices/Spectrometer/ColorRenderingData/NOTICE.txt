Numerical tables derived from Colour 0.4.7 (https://github.com/colour-science/colour/tree/v0.4.7).
CIE 13.3-1995 TCS01-14; optional TCS15 Japanese skin complexion:
https://doi.org/10.25039/CIE.DS.7chm7z5h (CIE 2024 data, JIS extension).
CIE 1931 2 degree CMFs: https://doi.org/10.25039/CIE.DS.xvudnb9b
CIE daylight basis: https://doi.org/10.25039/CIE.DS.w7zunnny
Robertson 1968 isotemperature lines from Colour temperature/robertson1968.py.
TCS and daylight tables are linearly interpolated to 1 nm, 380-780 nm.
Regeneration and independent reference fixtures: Test/Spectrum.Tests/ReferenceData/generate_color_rendering.py.
Runtime uses CIE 13.3 equations and no Python package. R15 is not included in Ra.
